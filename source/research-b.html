Bilbao

Check-in Sat 12 Sep · checkout Tue 15 Sep

3 nights
Worth it · conditional

Yes from Andra Mari stop; no from Eskerika

The direct A3631 meets the ~75-minute rule only if Saturday's locked finish is effectively Andra Mari stop 3416. If you actually stop around Eskerika, the extra 20–35 minute walk makes each transfer roughly 85–110 minutes, so it fails the rule.

Sat 12 Sep · outbound20:00 Andra Mari, Morga (stop 3416) → Bailén 1, Bilbao. Direct A3631, published route time about 60 min; allow 5–15 min to a central hotel.
Sun 13 Sep · return07:00 Bailén 1, Bilbao → Andra Mari, Morga. Direct A3631, about 60 min; scheduled to put you back at the stage point around 08:00.

Low-frequency warning: the through service has only three weekend trips in each direction. Missing Saturday’s 20:00 bus breaks the clean plan. Timetable researched 20 Aug 2026 from the current summer schedule; its accessible notice does not state an end date, so recheck 24–48 hours before travel.

Morga timetable Summer 2026 notice A3631 to Bilbao A3631 return Current stop times Eskerika walk Gernika E4 alternative

Why not Gernika? The official E4 is frequent and takes roughly 52 minutes from Gernika to central Bilbao, but first reaching Gernika from Andra Mari adds a rural bus/transfer. That pushes the practical trip beyond the direct A3631 and usually beyond the ~75-minute target.

All five Bilbao properties are central and offer private en-suite doubles, but public sources did not expose exact 12–15 Sep prices. Saturday inventory is the pressure point; every Booking and Airbnb button below carries the corrected three-night dates.
